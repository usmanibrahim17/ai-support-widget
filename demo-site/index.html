About Us

Baked with care, shared with love

Sweet Crumb Bakery started in 2016 as a tiny kitchen with one oven and a big love of sourdough. Today we're still a small, family-run bakery — every loaf, croissant, and pastry is made from scratch each morning using simple, honest ingredients.

No preservatives, no shortcuts — just slow fermentation, real butter, and recipes passed down and perfected over the years. Stop in for a fresh coffee and a warm pastry, or grab a loaf to take home for the week.
